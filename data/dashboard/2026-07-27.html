This domestic investment complements TSMC's substantial $265 billion expansion in the United States, aimed at meeting AI-driven demand and bolstering its advanced chip manufacturing leadership. The company is also boosting its EUV equipment production capacity by 30% from 2027 to address the growing AI chip demand, which is expected to spur further investment in EUV equipment, packaging, and advanced processes, making it challenging to gauge the industry's overall health solely through memory prices. Additionally, TSMC is further expanding its US investment by an additional $100 billion on top of its existing $165 billion, increasing the Arizona plant to 12 fabs and one R&D center. While the market is paying attention to the investment scale, concerns are also being raised that overseas expansion may pressure profitability.
